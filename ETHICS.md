# Ethics: consent, licences, labelling

## Consent

**No real person's face, likeness or voice appears in this film.** The characters (Almaz, the healer, the husband, the lion) are figures from a traditional Ethiopian folktale, drawn from text descriptions written for this project. They are not modelled on any real person, and no photograph of a real person was used as a reference.

The narration voice is a stock synthetic voice (Gemini TTS `Kore`, or Microsoft's `am-ET-MekdesNeural` if the Gemini quota ran out; `outputs/timeline.json` records which), not a clone of any real person's voice. Voice replication was not used.

If this changes (for example, a family member narrates or a relative's face is used as a reference), their recorded, informed consent goes here before anything of them is generated:

| Person | What is used (face / voice) | Words they agreed to | How consent was recorded | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | | | | |

## The story

*The Lion's Whisker* is a traditional Ethiopian folktale passed down orally. The narration in `storyboard.json` is my own retelling in Amharic and English, not copied from any published version.

## Labelling plan

- The film ends on a 4-second card: "Made with AI: images, narration and music were generated by AI models. Story retold by Abenezer Tilahun." (`end_card.ai_notice`; the schema refuses an end card without "AI").
- When posting, switch on the platform's AI disclosure (YouTube "altered or synthetic content", TikTok "AI-generated" label, Meta "AI info").
- Provenance metadata and watermarks from the providers are left intact; the pipeline does not strip metadata.

## Style and privacy

- The style is described by qualities (warm gouache, brush texture, highland palette), never by naming a living artist.
- No private family details were sent to any free endpoint; prompts contain only the folktale.
